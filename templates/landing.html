How it works inside a practice

When a patient cancels, SwiftSlot broadcasts the open slot to your consented short-notice list. The first confirmed response locks the appointment automatically.

1. Cancellation occursReception marks the slot as available and adds appointment details.
2. Immediate broadcastSwiftSlot notifies consented waitlist patients with a time-limited offer.
3. Slot securedThe first confirmed patient locks the appointment and the activity is tracked.
